The Big Picture — How Everything Connects

1
Content attracts visitors. Blog posts, the job board, and resources bring people to your site through search engines and social sharing.
2
Smart hooks capture emails. Every interaction — liking a post, reading a locked article, downloading a free resource — asks for an email address and adds it to your newsletter list automatically.
3
Digital products generate revenue. Products (templates, guides, courses) can be free (email-gated) or paid. Paystack handles all payments in Nigerian Naira (NGN). Download links are sent automatically after purchase.
4
Newsletters keep your audience engaged. You can send emails to your entire subscriber base, track who opens them and clicks links, and schedule sends in advance.
5
Analytics show you what's working. The dashboard gives you real-time stats — how many blog posts, products, jobs, orders, likes, and comments you have — all in one screen.

Feature-by-Feature Guide

Everything your platform can do, explained simply.

📰

Blog / Knowledge Centre

Your main content area. Publish articles on HR, career growth, workplace culture, leadership, and recruitment.

  • Write and publish articles with a visual editor (no coding needed)
  • Organise by category and tags
  • Add a featured image per post
  • Set any article as a Draft (hidden) or Published (live)
  • Lock the bottom half of any article behind a newsletter sign-up gate
  • Readers can like, dislike, and comment on posts
  • Easy share buttons on every article
📦

Resources & Digital Products

Sell or give away templates, guides, courses, and research reports — all delivered digitally.

  • Upload any file (PDF, Word, ZIP, etc.) securely to private storage
  • Set a price in Naira (NGN) or mark as Free
  • Free products require only an email address to access
  • Paid products use Paystack — the buyer pays, gets an email with a secure download link
  • Download links automatically expire after 7 days for security
  • Organise by category: Templates, Guides, Courses, Research
💼

Job Board

Post career opportunities for your audience to browse, filter, and apply to.

  • Add job title, company, location, job type, and experience level
  • Include salary range (optional)
  • Write the full description and requirements using the visual editor
  • Set an expiry date per listing
  • Applicants can apply via email or an external link
  • Visitors can search and filter by job type or experience level
📬

Newsletter & Email Marketing

Build and communicate directly with your audience via email.

  • Compose newsletters using a visual email editor
  • Schedule sends for a future date and time
  • See who opened your emails and which links they clicked
  • Subscribers are collected automatically from every sign-up point on the site
  • Every email has an automatic unsubscribe link (legally required)
  • Manage subscribers — view, filter by status (active, unsubscribed)
  • See where subscribers came from (article, product, footer form)
💬

Comments & Community

Let readers engage with your content through threaded comments on blog posts.

  • Readers can post comments and reply to each other
  • Comments are visible immediately when posted
  • You can Approve, Reject, Mark as Spam, or Delete any comment from the dashboard
  • Email address is optional for commenters (but helpful for follow-up)
🛍️

Orders & Payments

Track every product purchase in one place.

  • See all orders: customer email, product bought, amount paid, date
  • Each order has a unique Paystack reference for reconciliation
  • Order confirmation emails are sent to buyers automatically
  • All payments are processed in Nigerian Naira (NGN) via Paystack
📊

Dashboard & Analytics

A bird's-eye view of your entire platform at a glance.

  • Total blog posts, products, jobs, orders, comments, and likes
  • Newsletter performance: opens, clicks, and delivery stats
  • Google Analytics tracking built in — see traffic data in Google's dashboard
  • All data updates in real time as your audience interacts with the site
✉️

Contact Form

Let potential clients, partners, or readers reach you directly.

  • Visitors fill in their name, email, subject, and message
  • Message lands in your inbox immediately via email
  • Reply directly to the sender — their email is pre-filled as the reply address
